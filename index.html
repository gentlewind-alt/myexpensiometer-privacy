MyExpensiometer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026
App: MyExpensiometer (Android, package com.samarthrawat.myexpensiometer)
Developer: Samarth Singh Rawat
Contact: samarthrawat18@gmail.com

MyExpensiometer helps you record shared expenses and see who owes whom. It is built to work on your phone alone. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain words.

The short version

  • The developer does not collect, receive or store your data. There are no accounts and no servers run by the developer.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data sales.
  • The app's own code makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your phone

Only what you choose to enter or create:

  • Names of people you add, and the expenses, amounts, dates, labels and settlements you record.
  • The stories you type or dictate while using the Story feature (used only to create an expense).
  • Optional profile details you fill in yourself: name, phone, email, date of birth, gender, notes, photo, preferred currency and UPI ID.
  • App settings, such as reminder choices.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your phone.

How your stories are read

The Story feature turns a sentence such as "dinner 900 with Aarav and Meera, I paid" into an expense. This is done by a small language model that runs on your phone. Nothing you type is sent anywhere to be read.

The model file is delivered by Google Play when you install or update the app (as an asset pack), in the same way Play delivers any app file. After that, the app reads stories without using the internet.

Voice input

The microphone button on the Story screen opens Android's built-in speech recogniser (provided by Google or your phone maker). The app only receives the text that service returns. It does not record or keep audio. How the speech service handles audio is described in that provider's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show payment reminders, only if you turn them on.
  • Background work, wake lock, run at start-up and network state: used by Android's work scheduler so reminders and the optional daily backup can run on time and after a restart.
  • Internet: declared by Google's Play libraries that deliver the model file. The app's own code does not send your data over the internet.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. When you create a backup, export a file or pick a backup folder, Android's own file picker asks you where to save or what to open.

Sharing and exports

The app shares nothing by itself. Things only leave the app when you choose to:

  • Create backup / Export spreadsheet (CSV) / PDF statement: saved or shared through Android's share sheet or file picker, to a place you pick.
  • Automatic daily backup: saves a file to a folder you choose, and keeps the newest seven.
  • Pay via UPI or send a payment request: opens a UPI app, or shares a message or card, using Android's chooser. The app does not process payments and does not see whether a payment succeeds. Payments happen in your UPI app, under its own terms and privacy policy.

Android backup

Your phone may back up app data to your Google account if Android's backup is turned on. This is done by Android under your own Google settings, not by the developer. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

Children

MyExpensiometer is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The developer does not collect any information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove people and reverse entries in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
